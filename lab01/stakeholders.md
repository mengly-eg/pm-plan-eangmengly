# Stakeholders Overview

## High‑influence / High‑interest stakeholders
- **Head of the IT department (Sponsor)** – Drives budget approval and overall alignment with the department’s strategic goals. Their support is essential for securing resources and keeping the project on track.
- **Dr. Alice Smith (Project Manager / Supervising lecturer)** – Holds day‑to‑day authority, coordinates the student team, and ensures deliverables meet the academic and technical criteria.
- **Backend and Frontend Developer Teams** – Directly responsible for building the core platform. Their technical decisions strongly affect schedule and quality.
- **Pilot Students (50 end users)** – Provide real‑world usage feedback; their satisfaction determines the success of the MVP.

## Veto stakeholders
- **Data Protection Officer (Governance)** – Must sign‑off on privacy‑impact assessments and data‑handling policies. Any non‑compliance would block deployment.
- **IT Operations (Admin)** – Controls production environment access; refusal to accept the hand‑over plan would prevent go‑live.
